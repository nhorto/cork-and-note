## Recommendation

One App promotion campaign, one ad set, two active ads. Target US adults 21+, iPhone only, broad within those controls. Do not initially divide delivery by interests, age brackets, states, Facebook versus Instagram, or free versus paid positioning. Start with install optimization if the account and app support it; confirm the actual performance goal explicitly. Do not silently substitute link-click optimization.

Use highest-volume delivery initially, without an invented cost cap based on old website clicks. No separate awareness or retargeting campaign in month one. Keep platform/device and age restrictions as actual controls, not optional suggestions.

Use Facebook and Instagram feeds and Stories initially, subject to app-campaign placement availability. This is a deliberate starting choice for the available static artwork, not a claim these placements always outperform automatic placements. Supply proper 4:5 and 9:16 variants inside the same ad; they are not additional concepts. If the selected campaign configuration constrains placement controls, inspect actual previews and eligibility before committing. No unreviewed automatic rewrites or image changes.

## Geographic targeting decision

Launch across the United States rather than restricting the first campaign to Napa Valley, Sonoma County, or other wine regions. A wine-country location is an interesting intent signal, but it is imprecise: the audience includes residents and workers who may not want a wine-journal app, while interested visitors may be outside the selected radius when they plan a trip or remember a bottle. A separate regional ad set would also divide the $300 before the campaign establishes a usable national install-cost baseline.

Use wine-country relevance in the creative first. The winery-map challenger can show whether the winery-discovery benefit attracts installs while Meta still optimizes across the full eligible US audience. Do not add a second geographic campaign during month one merely to spend in famous wine regions.

Reconsider a regional test after the broad campaign has produced verified installs and a directional cost-per-install baseline. Run it as a small, separately budgeted experiment and compare it against the continuing broad audience during the same dates. Use the same winery creative and copy in both groups so geography is the main changed variable. Candidate regions include Napa/Sonoma first; expand to other wine regions only after the first geographic hypothesis has evidence. Treat the test as exploratory unless it generates enough installs to compare, and do not interpret cheap clicks as proof without installs.

## How to make decisions

Primary metric: spend / Meta-attributed installs, with the attribution definition and reporting window recorded. Keep first-time App Store downloads as a separate corroborating series; do not add the two reports or attribute all organic downloads to paid ads. An attributed install can represent first activation rather than Apple's download definition.

Secondary funnel: impressions → outbound clicks → store visits where reported → installs → first saved wine → return use → subscriptions. Activation/retention are quality checks when measurable, not prerequisites for the user's initial download goal. Do not promise per-ad downstream attribution unless implemented and verified.

- Check delivery, link, eligibility, spend and reporting within 24 hours. Fix broken destinations or incorrect device delivery immediately.
- Review about weekly. Allow for the configured iOS reporting delay before judging recent spend; avoid same-day winner declarations.
- Working risk trigger: if an ad spends $30 with zero attributed installs after reporting has caught up, pause that ad and investigate measurement/store conversion before spending more. This is a conservative budget rule, not statistical proof the ad cannot work.
- If both ads hit that trigger, pause the campaign. Establish whether the issue is measurement, store conversion, or the offer before resuming.
- With only one or two installs per ad, keep results tentative. Five or more installs can provide a directional comparison, still not a statistically reliable winner. Never call a barely served ad a loser.
- When both ads generate installs, favor lower install cost while checking any available evidence of first use. If cost is similar, keep both rather than force a winner.
- High click-through rate with few installs suggests checking the listing, promise, download availability and measurement. Cheap clicks alone do not qualify an ad to continue.

Scenario math, not forecasts: spending $300 yields 100 installs at $3 CPI, 60 at $5, 30 at $10, or 20 at $15. These are neither market benchmarks nor promises. A sustainable CPI cannot yet be inferred without conversion, retention and contribution-margin evidence. Do not raise the budget above $300 simply to force the platform out of a learning status.

## Launch readiness: resolve before relying on one-click publication

1. Confirm the published iPhone store listing is reachable and installable in the US. Approval and public availability are separate checks.
2. Identify the Meta developer app, iOS store ID/bundle ID, ad-account connection and eligible install optimization setup.
3. Complete the Meta developer-app setup, provide its App ID and Client Token as EAS build variables, then verify install/activation event receipt from a physical-iPhone build. The app-side Meta SDK and ATT consent boundary are now prepared in this checkout, but they remain disabled until both Meta values are supplied. No event has yet been verified against the real Meta account.
4. Ship the native integration in a later binary and review the affected App Store privacy answers with that binary. The version already in App Store review remains unchanged. A website pixel does not solve app-install attribution, and this native SDK cannot be added to the submitted binary through a JavaScript-only update.
5. App Store campaign links can supplement measurement where compatible with the chosen ad destination. They do not enable Meta install optimization by themselves and low-volume reports have reporting thresholds. Confirm the exact supported link setup.
6. Check the install objective, supported modern iOS delivery, US/21+ controls, spending cap, schedule, destination and both placement previews in the actual account.
7. Finish only the shortlisted vertical assets and final copy. Prepare paused drafts in a subsequent setup session, then inspect them before publication. Meta ad review can still delay delivery after publication.

Preferred response to missing install optimization: finish measurement readiness before committing the launch allocation. A separately discussed, small store-traffic diagnostic is a possible fallback, but must be explicitly labeled click-optimized and cannot be assessed as an install-optimized campaign.
