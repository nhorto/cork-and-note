Goal: make the Journal and Sommelier reachable without hiding the Cellar, lean into the winery/tasting identity, and stop resembling Sommo's bar (Today · Cellar · raised burgundy scan circle · Journal · Explore). Palette unchanged on purpose — this round is about structure. In every option, Profile leaves the bar (it lives on the Home avatar, top right, like every modern app) — that's the slot that frees the Journal.
