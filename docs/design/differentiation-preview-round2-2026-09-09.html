Re-theme round 2 — vibrant & editorial directions

Four new directions to compare against round 1 (A Midnight Cellar · B Cork & Vine · C Ink & Terracotta). D and E are the vibrant/fun end; F and G are the editorial/serious end. All four are rendered on the chosen IA — flat five-tab bar (Option 3) with the floating ＋ Log pill and the Passport home — so this is close to what would actually ship. Display type is a stand-in; the real face gets chosen with the palette.

D — “Spritz” (vibrant · warm)

Sunset coral + apricot on warm ivory, plum ink. Golden-hour patio energy — fun without going neon, and still food-and-wine warm. Furthest you can get from burgundy while staying warm.

E — “Juice” (vibrant · playful)

Grape violet + raspberry on a near-white lilac. Natural-wine-label, young-wine-club energy — the boldest personality change and the most social-media-friendly. Risk: may fight the refined Château voice.

F — “Broadsheet” (editorial · stark)

Near-black ink on paper white with one oxblood accent and hairline rules. The most serious option — a critic's field notebook. Maximum credibility, minimum decoration; nothing on the market looks like this.

G — “Ledger” (editorial · warm-serious)

Slate-navy + brass on warm paper. A sommelier's ledger: serious and premium but warmer than Broadsheet — brass does the work gold used to, without reading “wine cliché.” Strong dark-mode potential later.
