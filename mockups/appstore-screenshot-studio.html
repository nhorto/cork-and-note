Export sizes & a few useful details

Checked September 9, 2026 against Apple’s screenshot specifications and Google Play’s asset guidelines.

  • iPhone: 1320 × 2868 or 1290 × 2796 for the 6.9-inch slot; 1242 × 2688 for the 6.5-inch fallback. Apple accepts 1–10 screenshots per set.
  • iPad: 2064 × 2752 or 2048 × 2732 for the 13-inch slot. This app currently enables iPad support, so prepare actual iPad screenshots too.
  • Google Play phone: 1080 × 1920, 9:16. A listing needs at least two screenshots; four or more at this resolution supports eligibility for screenshot-based promotion. Up to eight per device type. The text region is smaller for this preset.
  • Exports use real 24-bit RGB PNG files without an alpha channel. Each file has the preset’s exact pixel dimensions, regardless of preview zoom or screen resolution.
  • The ZIP contains numbered PNGs plus a text manifest. Unzip it and upload the individual PNG files in order. The draft overview is watermarked and is not a store upload.

Google Play also needs a separate 1024 × 500 feature graphic and 512 × 512 app icon. This studio creates listing screenshots; those other assets are separate. Store review also considers the accuracy and quality of what you show.
