03 / From concepts to campaigns

Choose a few. Learn something useful.

These are six creative options to review. The first paid pilot should use only a small shortlist.

Step 1 · Choose

Pick two messages.

Start with remembering favorites and personal wine help. Choose one visual treatment for each, and record what you expect people to understand.

Step 2 · Make

Produce the final assets.

Generate the selected scene, add the approved copy and real app captures, then check feed and Story crops. Replace every scripted demonstration with a verified example.

Step 3 · Test

Follow the useful actions.

Before launch, measure qualified signups. Once the app is live, measure first saved wines and useful return visits alongside installs.
