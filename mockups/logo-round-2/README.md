# Cork & Note — Round 02

Open `index.html` in a browser. The gallery works directly from this folder, offline.

This round follows the selected direction: a Bordeaux square (#722F37) containing
an estate-gold circle (#C9A962), with the symbol or initials inside the circle.

- 01–04 refine The Tasting Nib, Estate Monogram, The Cork Seal, and Open Journal.
- 05–08 explore literal C & N lettering: classic serif, italic, stacked, and modern sans.
- Controls compare an outline ring with a filled gold medallion, flat with satin gold,
  and square with rounded outside corners. Square exports always retain the inner circle.
- `images/` contains four separately generated original raster PNG concepts (G1–G4).
- `vector-previews/` contains eight flat-gold/ring SVG masters and 1024 × 1024 PNG previews.
  Use the gallery to download other circle/finish combinations and sizes.
- `collection-preview.png` and `in-context-preview.png` are screenshots of the editable concepts.
- `PROMPTS.md` preserves the exact built-in image-generation briefs.

The generated images are fixed visual explorations; the gallery controls affect the
editable SVG collection. Generated textures and colors may vary from the exact SVG
palette. Lettered SVGs use local Georgia or Arial; after a direction is selected,
approved lettering can be outlined for consistent production use.

Favorites and notes are saved locally when browser storage is available. Download
feedback to retain or share it. Review copies can be found in Downloads under
`Cork and Note Logo Concepts/Round 2 - Bordeaux and Gold/`.

These are review concepts. The existing app assets and Expo configuration are not
replaced. The original first-round gallery remains available alongside this round.
